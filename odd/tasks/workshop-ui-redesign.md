## Specs
S1. "dale con todo si, aplica buenas practicas visuales para que el CRM tenga colores que ayuden con lo que es". Apply to the real CRM, not another standalone mockup. Visual reference: design/repair-status-board/01-colored-columns.html and design.json; map the design to React components rather than embedding the HTML.
S2. "si es un Kanban, que sea un Kanban de verdad"; "no sean contenedores". Open board without the large enclosing panel/nested bordered columns; readable cards, all active orders, real cross-stage movement and keyboard/touch equivalent. Preserve exact stored states/custom fallback and closed-order access. Same-column drop is a no-op, not invented persistent ranking.
S3. "el padding que hay en los laterales es mucho"; "cliente modalidad falla todo ese tipo de cosas tiene la misma jerarquía está todo super plano no se lee bien". Reduce lateral waste and top-align short views. Equipment/client get priority; code/mode/dates secondary. Group all existing detail information semantically, not sixteen equal grid cells.
S4. "lo que sí me gusta es en los botones que podemos el editar orden todo eso tiene pues un icono entonces está bien". Keep useful action icons, the actual React Bits sidebar and its seven icons/routes. Keep the warm existing palette/system fonts; no new libraries/fonts or fake component import.
S5. "se abre sin animación". Animated side sheet that does not reflow/deform the board, with internal scroll, meaningful focus/keyboard/close behavior and reduced-motion support. Preserve the originating anchor while showing a guarantee original, fresh displayed identity, and guarded in-flight actions.
S6. "pongo editar orden se ve como que en naranja"; "el cancelar edición y guardar cambios se ve también horrible y está a la izquierda". White fields, clear form sections and docked actions: Cancel secondary left, Save primary olive/white right. Preserve all 16 editable values, changed-fields-only patches, drafts/errors, validation, guarantee locks and successful persistence.
S7. "si pero los numeros iniciales que sean apalancamiento para tato... Y que se vea eso si en cajitas guay"; "si un poco, que sea tambien con graficos si pones en ver detalles, entende?". Compact KPI cards with prominent numbers and Ver detalles opening useful graphics from real Data: active repair distribution, recorded collections by period, priced pending balances by order, actual consultation states. Do not copy demo arrays or invent trends, conversions, profit, debt or records.
S8. "a si claro es la maqueta, no vie que era un html... Si si esta muchisimo mejor". The referenced prototype is the approved visual direction. Real application keeps its data/functions; prototype-only simulated writes are not production implementations.
S9. "quiero ver cambios rápido quiero ir cambiándolo y demás vale". Deliver visible cohesive UI changes promptly; use focused behavior RED/GREEN and one final integrated functional check, not full suites per cosmetic adjustment. This is not permission to skip required proof for changed data-writing interactions.
S10. "Veo la página igual de genérica en todos los módulos.". Carry semantic states and hierarchy into Consultas, Clientes, Órdenes, Finanzas, Repuestos and Crecimiento, without inventing urgency, inventory availability, real roadmap progress or permissions.
